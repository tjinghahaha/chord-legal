OpenStreetMap data in Chord

Chord shows nearby sacred and historic sites using data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 — openstreetmap.org/copyright.

How Chord’s extract is made

About once a month, Chord selects from the OpenStreetMap planet file every object that has one of the tags below, and loads them without changing the data. Nothing is added from other sources.

  • historic = holy_well, wayside_shrine, archaeological_site, ruins, memorial, monument, statue, castle, fort, city_gate
  • amenity = place_of_worship
  • building = cathedral, church, mosque, temple, shrine
  • natural = peak, volcano, cave_entrance, sacred
  • waterway = waterfall

The filter files, in machine-readable form: osm-tags.json and osm-denylist.json (empty: nothing is removed). The same data is available free from planet.openstreetmap.org. On request we will also send a copy of the extract itself, free of charge: daisy.apps.support+chord@gmail.com.
