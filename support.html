Common topics

Subscription & billing — Chord Plus is billed through your Apple account. To manage or cancel, open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Apple.

Restoring a purchase — On the Chord Plus screen, tap "Restore purchases".

Your data — You can export or delete your data anytime in Chord under Settings → Privacy. See our Privacy Policy.
