Privacy Policy

Chord · Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Chord collects, how it is used, and your rights. Chord is a daily reflection and entertainment app. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location data — Your approximate coordinates (Chord never asks for precise location), used only to compute local conditions: sun times and solar time, the sky over you, your season, weather and air quality, and nearby sacred and historic sites. To find those sites, your approximate location is sent to Chord's server for that lookup; Chord does not save it, and readings saved to your account do not include it. Any location data stored on our servers is deleted after 30 days. To tell a new place from a familiar one, Chord also keeps a rough record of where you opened it each day — about 5 km across, on this device only, never uploaded — and drops each entry after 30 days.
  • Birth information — Your date, time, and place of birth, provided voluntarily, used to personalize certain readings. Stored in your device's secure storage (Apple Keychain / Android Keystore). Chord's servers never receive it, and readings saved to your account do not include it. When you search for your place of birth, the text you type is sent to Apple's location service to find matching places. Clearing it in Settings removes it from every device where it appears.
  • Account information — Your account identifier: from Sign in with Apple; with Google Sign-In, the email address and name on your Google account; or your email address if you sign in with email. Signing in is optional; it is used to save your readings to your account. Without it, readings are not saved.
  • Purchase data — Whether you have Chord Plus, checked on your device with the App Store. Purchases are handled by Apple; no purchase history or payment details are sent to Chord’s servers or to any third party.
Chord works without an account and without providing any personal information. Personalization is optional—your reading simply draws from the surrounding conditions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute your personalized daily reading
  • To save your readings to your account while you are signed in
  • To process subscription purchases
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not show ads.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your birth information is stored only in your device's secure storage and is never sent to our servers. While you are signed in, your readings and account record are stored on Supabase infrastructure (United States / European Union). Chord does not save your location on our servers; any location data stored there is automatically deleted after 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Chord integrates with the following services:

  • Supabase — Cloud database & account authentication
  • Apple App Store — Subscriptions. Payment and purchase records stay with Apple; covered by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Sign in with Apple — Optional account sign-in
  • Google Sign-In — Optional account sign-in. Google shares your account’s email address and name with Chord; covered by Google’s privacy policy.
  • OpenWeatherMap — Weather & air-quality data. Only your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent; no name, account or device identifier. As with any web request, the provider can see your device’s IP address.
  • NOAA (SWPC & NCEI) — Geomagnetic data (no personal data sent)
  • Apple location services — Finding your place of birth when you search for it: the text you type is sent to Apple. Covered by Apple’s privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — Map data for nearby sites, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Chord keeps its own copy of this data; nothing about you is sent to OpenStreetMap.
  • Sentry — Anonymous crash diagnostics (no personal data, no user identity)

Of these data sources, only OpenWeatherMap receives a location (approximate, rounded); NOAA and OpenStreetMap receive nothing about you. No account or identifying information is sent to any of them.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your data (use "Export My Data" in Settings)
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data (use "Delete My Account" in Settings)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below or use the in-app options in Settings → Privacy.

6. Children's Privacy

Chord is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will note material changes here and update the date above. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
