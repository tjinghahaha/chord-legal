Privacy Policy

Chord · Effective Date: November 2026 · Version 1.9

Chord ("we", "our", or "us") is a daily reflection and entertainment app, and is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Location data — Your approximate coordinates (Chord never asks for precise location), used only to compute local conditions: sun times and solar time, the sky over you, your season, weather and air quality, and nearby sacred and historic sites. To find those sites, your approximate location is sent to Chord's server for that lookup; Chord does not save it, and readings saved to your account do not include it. As a safeguard, any location data that reaches our servers is deleted after 30 days. To tell a new place from a familiar one, Chord also keeps a rough record of where you opened it each day — about 5 km across, on this device only, never uploaded — and drops each entry after 30 days. So the home screen can show the stars over you when it has no fresh location, it also remembers the last place it drew them, about 5 km across, on this device only.
  • Birth information — Your date, time, and place of birth, provided voluntarily, used to personalise certain readings. Stored in secure storage on your devices: on Apple devices, Apple Keychain, which syncs it to your other Apple devices through iCloud Keychain when that is turned on (end-to-end encrypted by Apple); on Android, Android Keystore, on that device only. Chord's servers never receive it, and readings saved to your account do not include it. When you search for your place of birth, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places. Clearing it in Settings removes it from every device where it appears.
  • Account information — Your account identifier and email address: with Sign in with Apple, the email address Apple shares (your own, or a private relay address if you choose to hide it); with Google Sign-In, the email address and name on your Google account; or your email address if you sign in with email. Signing in is optional; it is used to save your readings to your account. Without it, readings are not saved.
  • Purchase data — Whether you have Chord Plus, checked on your device with the App Store. Purchases are handled by Apple; no purchase history or payment details are sent to Chord's servers or to any third party.
Chord works without an account and without providing any personal information. Personalisation is optional — your reading simply draws from the surrounding conditions. An email address is needed only if you create an account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute your personalised daily reading
  • To save your readings to your account while you are signed in
  • To check whether you have Chord Plus
  • To respond to support requests
  • To keep email sign-up and sign-in safe from automated abuse (hCaptcha)
  • To find and fix crashes, using reports that do not identify you (Sentry)

Under the GDPR and similar laws, we rely on your consent to use your location and birth details; on our agreement with you to run your account, save your readings, check Chord Plus and answer support requests; and on our legitimate interests in protecting the service from abuse and keeping it working, for security checks and crash reports.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not show ads. Chord does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so Do Not Track signals do not change how it works. Readings are generated automatically, but Chord makes no decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your birth information is stored only in secure storage on your devices (synced through iCloud Keychain when that is turned on) and is never sent to our servers. While you are signed in, your readings and account record are stored on Supabase infrastructure in the United States. Chord does not save your location on our servers; as a safeguard, any location data that reaches them is automatically deleted after 30 days.

Like any online service, Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs of requests to Chord's server — the time, your account identifier if you are signed in, your IP address and the approximate city it implies — for up to 7 days, for security and troubleshooting. These logs never contain your coordinates or birth information.

How long we keep data: your saved readings and account record stay until you delete them or your account. When you delete your account, your saved readings are erased at once and the account record within 30 days. Location data that reaches our servers is deleted within 30 days. Crash reports are deleted by Sentry within 90 days. Support emails are kept for up to 12 months after the conversation ends.

Chord is run from the United States, so your data is processed there, and some of the services in section 4 may process it in other countries. Supabase, which stores your account and saved readings, is bound by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in its data processing terms.

4. Third-Party Services

Chord integrates with the following services:

  • Supabase — Cloud database & account authentication
  • hCaptcha — A security check when you sign in, create an account or reset your password with email. hCaptcha receives your device's IP address and technical details about your device and how the check is used, to tell people from automated sign-ups; nothing about your readings. Not used with Sign in with Apple or Google. Covered by hCaptcha's privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store — Subscriptions. Payment and purchase records stay with Apple; covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Sign in with Apple — Optional account sign-in. Apple shares an email address with Chord (your own, or a private relay address); covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In — Optional account sign-in. Google shares your account's email address and name with Chord; covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • OpenWeatherMap — Weather & air-quality data. Only your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent; no name, account or device identifier. As with any web request, the provider can see your device's IP address.
  • NOAA (SWPC) — Live geomagnetic data (no location, account or device identifier sent)
  • GFZ Potsdam — Past geomagnetic Kp index, CC BY 4.0 (Matzka et al. 2021, doi:10.5880/Kp.0001); no location, account or device identifier sent
  • Apple Maps — Finding your place of birth when you search for it: the text you type is sent to Apple. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap — Map data for nearby sites, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Chord keeps its own copy of this data; nothing about you is sent to OpenStreetMap.
  • Sentry — Anonymous crash diagnostics (no account, name or user identity)

Of these data sources, only OpenWeatherMap receives a location (approximate, rounded), and OpenStreetMap is not contacted at all. No account, name or device identifier is sent to any of them; as with any web request, the ones Chord contacts can see your device's IP address.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your data (use "Export My Data" in Settings)
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data (use "Delete My Account" in Settings)
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interests (security checks and crash reports), or ask us to restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data, or by turning off location access for Chord in your device settings (this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before you withdraw)
  • Complain to a data protection authority, in particular where you live or work

To exercise these rights, contact us at daisy.apps.support+chord@gmail.com or use the in-app options in Settings → Privacy & Data.

6. Children's Privacy

Chord is meant for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us (section 8) and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make a material change to this policy — for example a new purpose, a new kind of data, a new recipient or a change to your rights — we will tell you in the app before the change applies to you, and update the dates on this page. Where a change needs your consent, the app will ask for it again; you can decline, withdraw consent or delete your account in Settings → Privacy & Data.

8. Contact
