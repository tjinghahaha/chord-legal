Privacy Policy

Chord · Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy explains what information Chord collects, how it is used, and your rights. Chord is a daily reflection and entertainment app. We aim to collect as little data as possible.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location data — Your approximate coordinates, used only to compute local environmental conditions (geomagnetic field, sunrise/sunset, nearby points of interest). Approximate location is not retained on our servers beyond 30 days.
  • Birth information — Your date, time, and place of birth, provided voluntarily, used to personalize certain readings. Stored in your device's secure storage (Apple Keychain / Android Keystore). Chord's servers never receive it. Clearing it in Settings removes it from every device where it appears.
  • Account information — Your account identifier: from Sign in with Apple, or your email address if you sign in with email. Signing in is optional; it is used to save your readings to your account. Without it, readings are not saved.
  • Purchase data — Subscription status, processed by RevenueCat. We never receive or store your payment card details.
Chord works without an account and without providing any personal information. Personalization is optional—your reading simply draws from the surrounding conditions.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To compute your personalized daily reading
  • To sync your readings across devices (only if you enable cloud sync)
  • To process subscription purchases
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not show ads.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your birth information is stored only in your device's secure storage and is never sent to our servers. While you are signed in, your readings and account record are stored on Supabase infrastructure (United States / European Union). Approximate location data is automatically deleted from our servers after 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Chord integrates with the following services:

  • Supabase — Cloud database & account authentication
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management
  • Sign in with Apple — Optional account sign-in
  • OpenWeatherMap — Weather & air-quality data (no personal data sent)
  • NOAA / NCEI — Geomagnetic data (no personal data sent)
  • OpenStreetMap — Points-of-interest data (no personal data sent)

The weather, geomagnetic, and map data sources receive only a coarse location parameter to return local conditions; no account or identifying information is transmitted to them.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region (including under GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to:

  • Access a copy of your data (use "Export My Data" in Settings)
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data (use "Delete My Account" in Settings)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below or use the in-app options in Settings → Privacy.

6. Children's Privacy

Chord is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We will note material changes here and update the date above. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
